Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

A.I agent is a personal tool operated by YOUR NAME. This page explains what information it handles.

Information the app handles

  • Account authorization: when the operator connects TikTok, the app receives an access token and a refresh token for the operator's own account. These are stored only on the operator's own computer.
  • Operator's videos and captions: video files chosen by the operator are sent to TikTok to create inbox drafts. Caption text is stored locally.
  • Basic profile information: the app requests basic profile access (user.info.basic) only to complete the login.

How information is used

Information is used only to upload the operator's own videos to the operator's own TikTok inbox and to operate the app. It is not sold, rented or shared for advertising.

Third-party services

The app sends data to TikTok to perform uploads. It also sends the operator's content briefs and caption drafts to Anthropic's Claude service to generate text. Those services handle data under their own privacy policies.

Storage and deletion

Data is kept on the operator's computer. Access can be removed at any time in TikTok under Settings → Security & permissions → Apps and services, or by deleting the app's saved login details from the operator's computer.
